Authority + Speaking: the room is the product

Buyer
A VP of People, chief of staff, or conference curator planning a consequential leadership event.
Sequence
event relevance → outcome standard → earned point of view → named method → speaking-topic fit → booking context.
Conversion contract
name, work email, organization, event date, audience size, format, budget range, and desired outcome. A generic “contact us” form cannot serve the motion.
Photography contract
hero presence, rehearsal / working scene, stage context, and editorial conversion portrait. A stage image is context, not speaking proof, unless it is permissioned.
