# AURA Compiler

## The premium personal-brand site engine

### From client evidence to a verified Astro release

## Slide 1

# A prompt is not a production system

A premium personal-brand website cannot start with a theme and a biography. It starts with a controlled position: a defined buyer, a costly tension, an approved offer, permissioned proof, visual intent, and one primary decision.

AURA Compiler turns those inputs into a traceable production path. It is deliberately designed to prevent generic page assembly, fabricated social proof, uncontrolled AI copy, and photography that implies access or results that do not exist.

> The engine compiles an approved strategic position; it does not improvise a website from a prompt.

## Slide 2

# One engine, seven connected stages

| Stage | Core question | Primary output |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence | What is true, approved and usable? | Client Brief and Evidence Register |
| Strategy | What commercial architecture fits this buyer? | Approved Strategy Record |
| Plan | What should the visitor understand and decide? | Page, CTA, proof and photo-role plan |
| Prompt | What may AI draft or direct? | Claim-constrained prompt packets |
| Compose | What can be rendered safely? | Typed Astro content candidates |
| Assets | What visual proof or art direction is required? | Shotlist and asset manifest |
| Verify | Does it work, remain true and render well? | Release report and visual QA |

Every stage may send the work back to strategy when a gate fails. No stage can silently publish an unresolved claim.

## Slide 3

# The client pack is the source of truth

Each client receives a portable pack with separate spaces for intake, strategy, assets, production, and release evidence.

| Pack area | What it holds | Why it is separate |
|---|---|---|
| `00-intake` | Buyer, tension, offers, evidence, voice constraints | Truth and commercial decisions are approved before production starts. |
| `01-strategy` | Architecture, rationale, page plan, decision log | The page sequence is selected deliberately rather than inherited from a theme. |
| `02-assets` | Consent, photo roles, crops, source status, derivatives | An image has a narrative job and a permission state—not merely a URL. |
| `03-production` | Prompt packets, Astro records, component plan | AI-assisted drafting remains inspectable and claim-constrained. |
| `04-release` | Validation, responsive QA, manifest and handoff | Publication is justified by evidence, not an informal “looks good.” |

## Slide 4

# Strategy selects an architecture—not a color palette

AURA uses six controlled architectures. Each changes the persuasion spine, proof standard, photography roles, conversion route, and form data.

| Architecture | Buyer motion | Primary conversion | Proof posture |
|---|---|---|---|
| Private Signal | High-discretion adviser / executive buyer | Private conversation | Confidentiality-aware method and permissioned outcomes |
| Authority + Speaking | Event buyer or leadership curator | Speaking availability | Topic outcomes, event context and organizer evidence |
| Creator + Education | Learner moving toward a program | Segmented offer route | Demonstrated learning and instructional proof |
| Niche Specialist | Vertical operator with a specific constraint | Diagnostic assessment | Case anatomy and operational proof |
| Manifesto + Movement | Identity-led audience | Application or community route | Real category relevance and doctrine |
| Portfolio + IP | Buyer evaluating a thesis across ventures | Chosen venture / IP path | Owned intellectual-property map |

## Slide 5

# Private Signal: discretion drives the experience

**Buyer:** a founder or senior operator who is capable, credible, and privately under-recognized.

**Sequence:** recognition → confidential tension → named method → considered proof → qualified fit → concierge conversation.

**Experience choices:** dark editorial atmosphere; unhurried type; a method artifact; working-scene evidence; restrained CTA cadence; no social-proof theatre.

**Photography contract:** a premium hero presence, method artifact, working scene, editorial close, and signature asset. Any synthetic frame stays clearly conceptual and never implies a confidential engagement.

## Slide 6

# Authority + Speaking: the room is the product

**Buyer:** a VP of People, chief of staff, or conference curator planning a consequential leadership event.

**Sequence:** event relevance → outcome standard → earned point of view → named method → speaking-topic fit → booking context.

**Conversion contract:** name, work email, organization, event date, audience size, format, budget range, and desired outcome. A generic “contact us” form cannot serve the motion.

**Photography contract:** hero presence, rehearsal / working scene, stage context, and editorial conversion portrait. A stage image is context, not speaking proof, unless it is permissioned.

## Slide 7

# Niche Specialist: diagnosis earns the next step

**Buyer:** a product or operating leader with a known commercial context and a delivery constraint that remains hard to locate.

**Sequence:** problem mirror → cost of inaction → named diagnostic → operational proof standard → scoped engagement → assessment.

**Conversion contract:** name, work email, company, business type, scale, timing, and service need. The form gathers enough context to decide whether diagnosis is appropriate.

**Photography contract:** hero presence, material decision-flow artifact, credible fieldwork, and conversation scene. The visual system makes the method inspectable without inventing dashboards or client access.

## Slide 8

# Role-based photography makes the page credible

AURA plans image roles before making or selecting images. This prevents the same portrait from being stretched across every section and prevents AI art from impersonating proof.

| Role | Visual job | Example archetype |
|---|---|---|
| P02 Hero Presence | Establish a capable, specific person with copy-safe space | All architectures |
| P09 Method Artifact | Make a named process tangible without fake readable data | Private Signal and Niche Specialist |
| P05 Working Scene | Show credible preparation or fieldwork without stock-laptop theatre | All proof-led architectures |
| P07 Stage Context | Support speaking-fit evaluation without fabricated credentials | Authority + Speaking |
| P08 Conversation Scene | Humanize a qualified working relationship without claiming a real client | Niche Specialist |

Every role carries consent, source state, desktop/mobile crop, alt-text requirement, generation packet, photographer alternative, and publication QA.

## Slide 9

# AI drafts inside evidence boundaries

The prompt compiler produces reviewable packets. Each packet includes the selected architecture, page role, allowed claim IDs, prohibited assertions, visual direction, output contract, and approval state.

The compose step does not fill a missing source fact with plausible language. It emits an explicit `[NEEDS APPROVAL]` placeholder. A claim can only be used publicly when its evidence register records source, permission, timeframe, scope, caveat, and allowed placement.

This is why AURA can use AI for structured acceleration without reducing a premium website to untraceable marketing output.

## Slide 10

# Dynamic pages and MDX serve different jobs

| Content type | Managed through | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Conversion pages | Typed JSON and Astro content collections | Home, method, services, speaking, contact, case study |
| Long-form thinking | MDX posts with validated frontmatter | Field Notes, Rehearsal Notes, Constraint Notes |
| Strategic updates | Client pack → reassessment → regenerated page plan | New offer, revised buyer, newly approved proof, changed CTA |

The archetype changes the editorial system. Private Signal publishes Field Notes that lead to a private conversation. Authority + Speaking publishes Rehearsal Notes that lead to availability checks. Niche Specialist publishes Constraint Notes that lead to diagnostic assessment.

## Slide 11

# Verification is part of the design system

AURA’s release compiler requires six connected gates.

| Gate | What blocks release |
|---|---|
| Truth | Unapproved, private, untraceable, or out-of-scope public claim |
| Strategy | Generic audience, missing primary decision, architecture mismatch, CTA sprawl |
| Sequence | Detailed offer pressure before method or proof without rationale |
| Assets | Missing consent, provenance, mobile crop, alt text, or optimized derivative |
| Implementation | Type/schema error, broken asset, missing H1, unsafe form endpoint |
| Experience | Mobile overflow, unusable crop, broken hierarchy, visual regression |

The current concept previews passed browser-level checks for HTTP status, image health, console errors, heading structure, and page-level desktop/mobile overflow.

## Slide 12

# The Studio can arrive without replacing the Compiler

The first version is intentionally file-driven, source-controlled, and deterministic. It has no standing AI cost, no opaque autonomous publishing path, and no client database requirement.

A future AURA Studio adds a friendly interface over the same contracts: discovery portal, evidence approval desk, strategy board, photo review board, content review, and release dashboard.

**The core principle remains unchanged:** the interface may become easier, but it must never bypass the evidence, strategy, asset, and release gates.
