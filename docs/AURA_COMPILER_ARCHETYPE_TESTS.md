# AURA Compiler Archetype Tests

This report records three **concept-safe** AURA Compiler runs. The tests prove that the engine changes the website plan when the commercial motion changes; it does not simply apply a visual theme to the same generic personal-brand template.

All three packs were validated with no blocking contract errors. They contain no claimed live clients, results, stages, media relationships or testimonials.

## How the home-page architecture changes

| Architecture | Commercial job | Home-page visitor-state sequence | What the page must earn before conversion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Private Signal** | Selective, confidential, high-touch authority advisory | Recognition → cost → conviction → trust → permission → action | A buyer must see their private tension, understand the authority method, and trust the confidentiality / proof standard before requesting a conversation. |
| **Authority + Speaking** | Event-buyer decision for a keynote, workshop or leadership intervention | Recognition → trust → earned authority → conviction → fit → action | An event owner must see relevance, credible format/outcome logic, the speaker’s earned point of view, and operational booking confidence before checking availability. |
| **Niche Specialist** | Narrow B2B diagnostic for a specific operating problem | Recognition → cost → conviction → trust → fit → action | An executive must recognize a vertical-specific operational constraint, understand the diagnostic method, and know what qualification context is needed before starting an assessment. |

The first and third structures look superficially similar because both have a problem and a method. Their conversion logic is different. Private Signal aims for a considered private conversation, while Niche Specialist aims to collect the diagnostic variables that establish whether a precise B2B engagement is relevant.

## Conversion and form-contract comparison

| Architecture | Primary CTA | Required form context | Secondary action | What the engine blocks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Private Signal** | Request a private conversation | Name, email, company, decision context, timing, desired outcome | See how the work unfolds | A generic contact form that treats a confidential advisory decision as a low-context lead. |
| **Authority + Speaking** | Check speaking availability | Name, email, organization, event date, audience size, format, budget range, desired outcome | Explore keynote outcomes | A booking CTA without event operating context or a proof system that implies fictional stages. |
| **Niche Specialist** | Start a decision-flow assessment | Name, email, company, business type, scale, timing, service need | See the diagnostic method | Broad “work with me” language that does not qualify a vertical-specific operating issue. |

## Photography-plan comparison

| Role | Private Signal | Authority + Speaking | Niche Specialist |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P02 — Hero presence** | Cinematic private-room authority with copy-safe negative space. | High-contrast leadership presence; can be event-adjacent without pretending a real stage. | Clear operator portrait in an analytical product / operating world. |
| **P05 — Working scene** | Evidence of serious private work or a material advisory artifact. | Preparation, rehearsal, or leadership work that supports the speaking method. | Credible product-operations analysis or workshop activity; never generic laptop stock imagery. |
| **P06 — Editorial close** | Calm private advisory conversion portrait. | Considered end-of-page speaker close that supports the availability CTA. | Not required by the profile; the assessment route should use operational evidence instead. |
| **P07 — Stage / room context** | Not required; stage context may weaken a private-advisory frame. | Required at the speaking offer moment; must be real or explicitly labelled conceptual. | Not required; the buyer needs operational context, not performance-stage status. |
| **P08 — Conversation scene** | Not required; private work can be shown through a more discreet artifact or working scene. | Not required; workshop visual can be P05 or P07 depending on truth state. | Required at the offer stage to show a diagnostic conversation / operating review without implying a real client relationship. |
| **P09 — Method artifact** | Required to make the Quiet Authority Method tangible. | Optional; the framework may be better carried through speaker outcomes and rehearsal context. | Required to make the Decision-Flow Diagnostic concrete and distinguish it from generic process consulting. |
| **P12 — Signature asset** | Required as the final concierge conversion surface. | Not required; operational booking detail is more useful at the close. | Not required; assessment qualification is the right close. |

The plan deliberately varies photo roles. It avoids the common pattern where a single portrait is reused as hero, method, proof and final CTA decoration.

## Prompt packets produced per architecture

Each compile creates four auditable prompt classes under `clients/<slug>/03-production/prompts/`.

| Packet | Private Signal emphasis | Authority + Speaking emphasis | Niche Specialist emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|
| `01-positioning.md` | Convert private capability into legible authority without relying on loud visibility. | Link high-stakes communication to a rehearsable leadership behavior. | Reframe delivery delay as a decision-system constraint. |
| `02-page-plan-review.md` | Check confidential-proof posture, fit and concierge CTA pacing. | Check organizer proof, talk outcomes and booking-process completeness. | Check diagnostic proof, method order, assessment qualification and offer specificity. |
| `page-*.md` | Draft only with the Quiet Authority claim IDs and private-work scope. | Draft only with framework and booking-policy claims; no fictional stage or event proof. | Draft only with diagnostic and assessment-policy claims; no fictional case metrics. |
| `03-photo-direction.md` | Private-room, artifact and conversion-close composition. | Stage / room context, rehearsal and event-buyer authenticity safeguards. | Operational fieldwork, decision artifacts and non-deceptive conversation context. |

## What the test proves

The Compiler changes all of the following from the selected architecture: page count, home visitor-state sequence, form schema, CTA wording, evidence posture, supporting-route map, photography roles, component candidates, prompt constraints, and content-output provenance.

The test does **not** claim that the two new fictional concepts are published client sites. They are local engine test packs, deliberately excluded from the public repository along with all future real client packs.
